Unsent: The Micro-Confession Sprint

A two-part micro-confession typed and never sent — to my little brother, the guilt of ten years, blamed on the neighbour's dog.

To: my little brother
I was the one who broke your bike the summer before you left for college, and I let you blame the neighbour's dog for ten years.
